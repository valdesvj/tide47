REM TIDE47 v0.1.0 - Tide 47: tide predictions for the C47 / R47 (and the DM42n with the C47 firmware)
REM The harmonic method of NOAA and the Admiralty (Schureman, US C&GS Special Publication 98):
REM   h(t) = Z0 + sum f H cos(V + u - g + w t)
REM over 60 constituents (NOAA's 37, 8 of TICON's, 15 for the Aramco tables), with the 18.6-year nodal corrections f and u, as whole
REM matrices (the firmware works on every element: SIN COS LN e^x, M + M, M - M, M x r, M MOD r):
REM   P = DM x AV + UM x BV - G  (MOD 360)     the phases at the day's start: DM the Doodson numbers (60 x 7), AV
REM                                            the angles tau s h p N' p1 90; UM the u combinations, BV xi nu nu'
REM                                            2nu'' Q R p
REM   A = e^(EM x LF + LH)                     the amplitudes f H: EM the node-factor powers (60 x 10), LF the logs of
REM                                            the 10 basic factors, LH the logs of the port's H (no loop for f x H)
REM   h(t) = Z0 + A . COS(W t + P)            one height: one COS of a 60-vector and one DOT
REM High and low waters: the hourly signs of h'(t) = -AW . SIN(W t + P), then Newton (h'' = -AW2 . COS) in the
REM hour. python/tide47ref.py is the same, step for step (tests/test_tide47.py compares them).
REM The ports are in the data programs TIDEME, TIDEUS, TIDELA (load all three): XEQ "TIDEME" with X = the port's
REM number sets its name (R26), time zone (R27), DST (R28: 1 = US rules), datum (R29), source (R95), Z0 (R24) and
REM the matrix HG; X = 0: R96 the number of ports, R97 the region.
REM Keys: 4 6 the day before / after, 1 3 the week before / after, 5 today, 8 2 the port before / after, + the next
REM region (MIDDLE EAST, USA, LATIN AMERICA), 0 the end.
REM Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = the bottom: the header at 220 and 210, the chart at x 26-386 (15 pixels an hour), rows
REM 118-196 (the time axis at 115), the table rows 0-100.
REM Registers: R20 the region, R21 the port, R22 the day's start (JD, UT), R23 the time zone of the day (DST in),
REM R24 Z0, R25 the day (JDN of the local date), R26-R29 R95-R97 the port, R30-R39 R50-R59 the astronomy, R40-R47
REM the calendar, R50-R58 the events' search, R60-R65 the event times (hours), R66-R71 their heights, R72-R77 their
REM kinds (1 high, -1 low), R78 how many, R80 R81 the chart's lowest and highest heights, R82 its step, R83 pixels a
REM metre, R84 R87-R91 R93 drawing, R85 today (JDN), R86 the hour now, R92 1: load the port, R98 the key.
REM The user's stack and LASTx are kept (LocR 9: STOS R.00, LASTx in R.08); R00-R19 are not touched; at the end
REM R20-R99 are cleared and the matrices deleted; the angle mode is left in DEG.
