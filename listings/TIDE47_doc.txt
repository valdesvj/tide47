      ; TIDE47 v0.3.0 - Tide 47: tide and tidal-stream predictions for the C47 / R47 (and the DM42n with the C47 firmware)
      ; The harmonic method of NOAA and the Admiralty (Schureman, US C&GS Special Publication 98):
      ; h(t) = Z0 + sum f H cos(V + u - g + w t)
      ; over 60 constituents (NOAA's 37, 8 of TICON's, 15 for the Aramco tables), with the 18.6-year nodal corrections f and u, as whole
      ; matrices (the firmware works on every element: SIN COS LN e^x, M + M, M - M, M x r, M MOD r):
      ; P = DM x AV + UM x BV - G  (MOD 360)     the phases at the day's start: DM the Doodson numbers (60 x 7), AV
      ; the angles tau s h p N' p1 90; UM the u combinations, BV xi nu nu'
      ; 2nu'' Q R p
      ; A = e^(EM x LF + LH)                     the amplitudes f H: EM the node-factor powers (60 x 10), LF the logs of
      ; the 10 basic factors, LH the logs of the port's H (no loop for f x H)
      ; h(t) = Z0 + A . COS(W t + P)            one height: one COS of a 60-vector and one DOT
      ; High and low waters: the hourly signs of h'(t) = -AW . SIN(W t + P), then Newton (h'' = -AW2 . COS) in the
      ; hour. python/tide47ref.py is the same, step for step (tests/test_tide47.py compares them).
      ; The ports are in the data programs TIDEME, TIDEUS, TIDELA, TIDECU (load those you want: TIDE47 finds them, LBL?):
      ; XEQ "TIDEME" with X = the port's number sets its name (R26), time zone (R27), the kind (R28: + 1 = US daylight
      ; time; + 2 a rotary stream, + 4 a rectilinear stream), datum (R29; a stream: its units and directions), source
      ; (R95), Z0 (R24) and the matrices HG (HV); X = 0: R96 the number of ports, R97 the region, R93 the data version.
      ; Tidal streams (R28 >= 2): HG the constants of the speed along the flood direction (rectilinear, knots, + flood,
      ; - ebb; the variable SDIR = the flood direction + the ebb's / 1000) or of the east component u (rotary: then HV
      ; the north component v, SDIR its mean V0). The events are the turning points of the speed: g = u u' + v v'
      ; (= s s') changes sign: the maxima (flood / ebb, MAX) and the minima (slack, MIN), with the set (the direction the
      ; stream goes, deg true). tide47ref.sday is the twin.
      ; Keys: 4 6 the day before / after, 1 3 the week before / after, 5 today, 8 2 the port before / after, + the next
      ; region loaded (MIDDLE EAST, USA, LATIN AMERICA, US STREAMS), 0 the end.
      ; Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = the bottom: the header at 220 and 210, the chart at x 26-386 (15 pixels an hour), rows
      ; 118-196 (the time axis at 115), the table rows 0-100.

      ; R24 Z0, R25 the day (JDN of the local date), R26-R29 R95-R97 the port, R30-R39 R50-R59 the astronomy, R40-R47

      ; metre, R84 R87-R91 R93 drawing, R85 today (JDN), R86 the hour now, R92 1: load the port, R98 the key.
