      ; TIDE47 v0.1.0 - Tide 47: tide predictions for the C47 / R47 (and the DM42n with the C47 firmware)
      ; The harmonic method of NOAA and the Admiralty (Schureman, US C&GS Special Publication 98):
      ; h(t) = Z0 + sum f H cos(V + u - g + w t)
      ; over 54 constituents (NOAA's 37, 8 of TICON's, 9 for the Aramco tables), with the 18.6-year nodal corrections f and u, as whole
      ; matrices (the firmware works on every element: SIN COS LN e^x, M + M, M - M, M x r, M MOD r):
      ; P = DM x AV + UM x BV - G  (MOD 360)     the phases at the day's start: DM the Doodson numbers (54 x 7), AV
      ; the angles tau s h p N' p1 90; UM the u combinations, BV xi nu nu'
      ; 2nu'' Q R p
      ; A = e^(EM x LF + LH)                     the amplitudes f H: EM the node-factor powers (54 x 10), LF the logs of
      ; the 10 basic factors, LH the logs of the port's H (no loop for f x H)
      ; h(t) = Z0 + A . COS(W t + P)            one height: one COS of a 54-vector and one DOT
      ; High and low waters: the hourly signs of h'(t) = -AW . SIN(W t + P), then Newton (h'' = -AW2 . COS) in the
      ; hour. python/tide47ref.py is the same, step for step (tests/test_tide47.py compares them).
      ; The ports are in the data programs TIDEME, TIDEUS, TIDELA (load all three): XEQ "TIDEME" with X = the port's
      ; number sets its name (R26), time zone (R27), DST (R28: 1 = US rules), datum (R29), source (R95), Z0 (R24) and
      ; the matrix HG; X = 0: R96 the number of ports, R97 the region.
      ; Keys: 4 6 the day before / after, 1 3 the week before / after, 5 today, 8 2 the port before / after, + the next
      ; region (MIDDLE EAST, USA, LATIN AMERICA), 0 the end.
      ; Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = the bottom: the header at 220 and 210, the chart at x 26-386 (15 pixels an hour), rows
      ; 118-196 (the time axis at 115), the table rows 0-100.
      ; Registers: R20 the region, R21 the port, R22 the day's start (JD, UT), R23 the time zone of the day (DST in),
      ; R24 Z0, R25 the day (JDN of the local date), R26-R29 R95-R97 the port, R30-R39 R50-R59 the astronomy, R40-R47
      ; the calendar, R50-R58 the events' search, R60-R65 the event times (hours), R66-R71 their heights, R72-R77 their
      ; kinds (1 high, -1 low), R78 how many, R80 R81 the chart's lowest and highest heights, R82 its step, R83 pixels a
      ; metre, R84 R87-R91 R93 drawing, R85 today (JDN), R86 the hour now, R92 1: load the port, R98 the key.
