---- What it is ----
{LABEL} is a data program of Tide 47: the harmonic constants of the {N} ports of the region {TITLE}, for TIDE47 to
predict their tides. The ports: {PORTS}.

---- How to use it ----
Load it with TIDE47 and press XEQ TIDE47 (not XEQ {LABEL}: TIDE47 calls it). Load only the regions you want:
TIDE47 finds the data programs on the calculator, and + goes from one region to the next. Load the {LABEL} built
together with your TIDE47: an older one is left out with OLD DATA, REBUILD.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Called by TIDE47 with a port's number, it sets the port's name, time zone, datum, source and mean level in TIDE47's
registers and writes the matrix HG (one number a constituent); TIDE47 deletes HG at its end. Nothing else.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program with one global label ({LABEL}); each port is a local label (LBL 01-{N}), LBL 99 the region.
