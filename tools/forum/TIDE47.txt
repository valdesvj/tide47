---- What it is ----
Tide 47 predicts the tide on the C47 / R47: for any day at a port, the times and heights of the high and low waters
and the tide curve of the day, worked out on the calculator by the harmonic method that NOAA's and the Admiralty's
tables use (60 constituents with the 18.6-year nodal corrections, as whole matrices). The ports come in data
programs by region: TIDEME (the Middle East: Bahrain, Oman, Iran, Pakistan, Yemen, Djibouti), TIDEUS (ten US ports)
and TIDELA (Latin America). Heights are in metres above the chart datum (LAT, or MLLW for the US ports).
NOT FOR NAVIGATION: the astronomical tide only (wind, pressure and surges add to it); the official tables of the
port authority are the reference.

---- How to use it ----
Load TIDE47 and the data programs of the regions you want (one is enough: TIDE47 finds those loaded), then press
XEQ TIDE47. Setting up takes about 8 seconds; it shows today at the first port. The day is the calculator's date;
the dotted line on the curve is its clock (set the calculator to the port's local time).
  4 6   the day before / after            1 3   the week before / after          5   today
  8 2   the port before / after           +     the next region loaded           0   the end
The screen: the port, the date, its time zone, the datum and the source; the day's curve (hours 00-24) with each
high and low water marked; the table of the high and low waters with their heights and ranges. A new day or port
takes about 3.5 seconds. A data program from an older version is left out with OLD DATA, REBUILD.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack and LASTx are given back at the end. Registers R00-R19 are not touched; R20-R99 are used and cleared at
the end. It makes its matrices while it runs (DM UM EM SPD W LW ONES AV BV LF HG HH G LH P L A AW AW2 Z HS) and
deletes them at the end. The angle mode is left in DEG; GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20 are set.

---- Programs and labels ----
TIDE47 is one program with one global label (TIDE47); every routine is a local label. Each data program is one
program with one global label (TIDEME, TIDEUS, TIDELA) and a local label per port. Written with the STRUCT commands,
the matrix functions, ATEXT and GRFNT of the C47 / R47 firmware (00.109.05.00a0.ALPHA of 5 Oct 2026 or later); LN
and LBL? are used here for the first time on a calculator (see the README).
